About

How's it going?! I'm Salvador, a Master's student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin. I've interned as a Machine Learning Engineer at Pinterest and Uber and twice as a Software Engineer at Google, and I enjoy doing research! So far I've published five peer-reviewed papers as a first author, with one more under review (going from Theoretical ML to Fairness and Responsible AI). I hold dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics from The University of Texas at El Paso with a concentration in Statistics and Data Analytics.

Research interests: Multimodal AI (Vision + Text), Natural Language Processing, Agentic AI, and Responsible AI (e.g. Alignment).

Hobbies: Soccer, chess, weightlifting, reading Dostoevsky (favorite: The Brothers Karamazov), and sustainable gardening.
